YouTube description (full)
--------------------------
Workspace Clone is a peer-to-peer desktop app for cross-device work-session
continuity. Start work on one machine and it captures a description of your
environment — open projects and their git branches, apps, runtimes, env vars,
accounts, and your uncommitted git changes — then rebuilds that setup on your
other laptop over a direct encrypted connection.

How it works
- No server and no cloud account: the two machines talk directly (mDNS +
  Noise-encrypted, key-authenticated transport).
- Nothing happens without your approval: every restore step is explained in
  plain English before it runs.
- Git history survives the trip: cloned repos are re-synced with your
  working-tree delta re-applied, so `git status` shows exactly your changes.
- macOS and Windows, with installers for both.
